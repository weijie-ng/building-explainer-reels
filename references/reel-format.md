# Reel format: the reference reel

The style target for this skill. Use it to plan a new reel's beats and to review a build against the reference. The reference reel is a vertical 9:16, 80-second investigative explainer about FIFA's finances.

## Reporting beats

| Beat | What the reference reel does |
|---|---|
| Thesis-first hook | "The King of FIFA" launched a money-making scheme, and it involves Donald Trump. |
| One chain (money) | FIFA, officially a non-profit, spins its commercial side into a separate $20bn business; outside investors buy up to a 20% stake; the reported lead investor is Joshua Kushner, whose brother is married to Trump's daughter. |
| Motive | Infantino stays in power by winning FIFA's 211 member associations; the new entity could pay them about $2m each to start, then $3m a year. |
| Anecdotes | A custom "FIFA Peace Prize" for Trump; a disciplinary ban on a US player lifted after White House pressure; the US chosen to host the 2031 Women's World Cup. |
| Funnel | "Watch the full deep dive", pointing to the long-form video, then a branded subscribe card. |

Cautions:
- **Figures:** the $20bn, 20% and $2m/$3m figures appear without named sources in the reel's description, so verify them independently before reusing any of them.
- **Tone:** "scheme", "tearing football apart" and "cozy up" signal an argument rather than a balanced report.

## Visual grammar

- **Presentation.** Voiceover only; no presenter or avatar. People appear only as subjects in photos and event footage.
- **Palette and type.**
  - Colour: saturated blue backgrounds, one dark-green "explanation" section (the member-association diagram; this skill uses a single blue background throughout instead), and white headlines with single-word yellow highlights.
  - Type: a tight geometric sans for headlines; monospace for subtitles.
- **Article receipts.** A recreated headline on blue: small outlet name (Financial Times, The Guardian, Yahoo Sports), large headline, a thin line with a dot tracing down to faint body text, and the key phrase highlighted. Faces and photos are added to the card as the narration names people.
- **Photos.** Full-bleed photos alternate with inset rounded-rectangle photos and clips; cut-out faces sit on maps.
- **Maps and diagrams.** A semicircle diagram of member associations by confederation with a face at its centre, plus world and North America maps with face cutouts and a trophy icon. Numbers appear as on-screen text or inside article cards, never as invented charts.
- **Subtitles.** Burned in, 2–5 words, white bold monospace in a tight black box, near the middle of the lower third, changing phrase by phrase.
- **End card.** The long-form video's thumbnail with a cursor and "watch the full deep dive", then a near-black brand card with a subscribe button.

## How it moves

Measured frame by frame, the reel is in some kind of motion almost all the time:

| Frame state | Share of frames |
|---|---|
| Fully still | 12% |
| Slow creep (small change every frame) | 53% |
| Clear movement (a move in progress) | 28% |
| Cut or fast transition frame | 7% |

Subtitles never move. Everything else does, in these distinct moves (times are from the reference reel):

| Move | What happens | Example | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slow creep | The card, map or photo pushes in or drifts upward slightly; the card text moves with its card, the subtitles stay put | Every headline card; photos between cuts | The whole shot |
| Graphic wipe | A themed object sweeps across the frame, covering the old shot and revealing the new one | A stack of banknotes rises from the bottom over a portrait to reveal the next photo (1.1–2.6s); a football flies up across Trump to reveal Infantino (4.4–4.8s) | 0.3–1.5s |
| Particle overlay | Objects fall over a photo for its whole shot | Gold confetti over Infantino and Trump (1.9–4.5s) | ~3s |
| Pan across a wide photo | A wide event photo slides past to reveal more of it | A congress hall with rows of flags (6.6–7.9s), then a cut | ~1.3s |
| Fade-in on a card | A photo fades in from transparent onto the headline card as the person is named; later photos join beside it | Joshua Kushner's photo appears on "billionaire investor" (14.8–15.1s); a family photo joins on "married to Trump's daughter" | ~0.4s each |
| Line-and-dot extend | The tracing line grows to the next quoted sentence as the narration moves on | FT card, Guardian card, Yahoo card | ~0.3s |
| Slide-up into place | A face cutout rises from below into the centre of a diagram | Infantino's face rises into the semicircle (25.6–26.0s) | ~0.4s |
| Camera zoom on a graphic | The view zooms into the diagram, then back out, while tokens flow | Member-association semicircle (30.0–33.7s) | ~3.7s |
| Token flow | Small "$" tokens stream from the centre to each region of the diagram on "pay out" | Semicircle, 30–33s | During the zoom |
| Map move with pop-ins | The map pans between places as they are named; faces and a trophy icon pop in at each | Russia → Qatar → Saudi Arabia (47.8–51.6s), then a cut to North America | ~1s per move |
| Inset clip | Real footage plays inside a rounded card on blue, cutting between clips | Argentina/USA cap clip, trophy handover (70.2–74.1s) | 1–2s per clip |
| Real footage | Event video plays full frame | The medal ceremony (54.8–58.4s) | 1–4s |
| Cursor click | A cursor moves onto the long-form video's thumbnail and clicks | The funnel (74.2–76.8s) | ~2.5s |
| Word-by-word colour | The closing line's words change colour one at a time | The end card (76.8–80s) | ~3s |
| Hard cut | Most changes between shots happen on a narrated word | Throughout; about 33 changes in 80s | instant |

What it does not do: no whole-frame zoom or pan on top of the subtitles, no motion on the subtitle boxes, and no long holds with nothing changing.

| Measure | Reference reel |
